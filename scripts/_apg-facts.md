## Writing and evidence

Write direct, useful prose with specific sourced examples. No em dashes, en dashes, rhetorical questions, repeated sentence openings or false-negative framing. Avoid artificial slogans and unsupported authority claims. Use natural conjunctions and varied sentence lengths. Do not use bold, italic or underlined emphasis in body copy.

A brief is an instruction to research, not evidence. Only use verified sources provided with it. Preserve historical publication dates and do not rewrite a past year to the current year. When evidence is missing, omit the claim or leave the draft for review. Every article remains a Notion Draft until editorial approval.
