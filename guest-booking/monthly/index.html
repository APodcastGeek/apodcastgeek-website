How it works

There are two ways to work with us. You can approve every guest we find, or let us book suitable guests without approval. Choose your option in the pack box above.

1

2

3

4

5

  1. Packs are 2, 3 or 4 guests. Your first guest is free, and each guest after that is €250. The free guest applies to your first pack only.
  2. We send an invoice once you submit this form. Payment is due upfront, and we start work when it's paid.
  3. Once work has started, paid guests are not refundable if you change your mind. The only refund is the guarantee in point 4.
  4. If we can't book a guest in time for their episode, we refund the €250 for that guest. This needs your current, up-to-date recording availability, and it doesn't apply to the free guest.
  5. We go after the guests on your list first. We can't promise any named person will say yes. When they don't, we find other guests who suit your audience. If you chose to approve guests, you approve every guest before we reach out.
  6. If you chose to approve guests, please review your shortlist within 5 working days. Our booking times start from your approval.
  7. If a booked guest cancels or doesn't show up, we find you a replacement at no extra cost. If you cancel or reschedule a recording, or don't reply to us about it for 10 working days, that guest counts as delivered.
  8. You give us your recording booking link, or access to however you book recordings, so we can see your availability and book guests into open slots. Please keep it up to date.
  9. Unused guests in a paid pack can be used for up to 3 months from payment.
  10. We contact guests on your behalf using your podcast name, and every guest signs our guest release form before recording.
  11. Prices are in Euro and exclude any VAT that may apply.
