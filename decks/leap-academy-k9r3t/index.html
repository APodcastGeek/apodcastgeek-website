And this is the part that decides today

Brand Builder eligibility.

1
Strategy is not up for debate
We require control and cooperation with the strategies we create for your podcast. Specifically, guest intros, clip selection, titles, thumbnails, packaging, distribution and media buying are ours to control. If that is not something you are comfortable with, you would not be applicable for the Brand Builder programme.
2
One voice on the account
We need clear, concise communication from one person only for this account. Multiple voices chiming in after deliveries are made, or after revisions are done, are not acceptable.
3
Respect our process
Our revisions policy is up to 48 hours per revision request. On occasion, where we have the bandwidth, we may be able to accommodate sooner. Notably, on the Brand Builder we would be working two to four episodes in advance at all times, so the pressure around releases is not present and revision windows do not carry that added pressure. This is a requirement for the programme.
4
Pre-production on every episode
The APG episode brief template is used in pre-production for every episode. Pre-production makes up roughly 70% of what makes a successful podcast successful. With that intention behind your recordings, post-production, management and growth all become far easier.
If you are happy to accommodate the above terms in working with us, that would make you eligible for the Brand Builder programme. Our full terms will be included in the contract we send over. If a client breaches the terms, we send written notice of the breach and you get 14 days to fix it. If it is not fixed in that time, we then serve 30 days written notice to terminate the agreement.
