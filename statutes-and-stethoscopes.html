About Tracy

Tracy Liberatore doesn't just bridge the gap between medicine and law. She built the bridge. As the founder of Med Legal Pro LLC, Tracy brings over two decades of hands-on medical experience and nearly a decade of legal expertise to the table. A top-10% graduate of Marywood University's PA program, she's practiced in ENT, Head and Neck Surgery, Hematology-Oncology, Psychiatry, and Urgent Care. Her fluency in electronic medical records makes her an asset in today's litigation landscape. In 2016, Tracy graduated valedictorian from law school, earning high honors and accolades like the Jack R. Goetz and Legal Services Awards. She's been a proud member of the California Bar ever since. At Med Legal Pro, she leads with clinical precision and legal acumen, helping law firms decode complex medical issues, craft compelling arguments, and deliver bulletproof case strategy. With a 30-day turnaround and a network of rigorously vetted experts, Tracy ensures every case gets the attention and expertise it deserves. Beyond consulting, Tracy has trained clinicians for over 10 years to become effective medical-legal experts. Through the National Expert Academy, she offers a robust curriculum in expert report writing, live testimony training, personalized 1:1 coaching, and destination-based retreats focused on courtroom communication, credibility, and impact.
