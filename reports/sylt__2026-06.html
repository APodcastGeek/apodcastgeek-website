Production Notes
What's Working

June's episode with Brett Rose Pilar of One Block Las Vegas pulled in more than double your usual episode reach, and 65% of that traffic came from YouTube recommending the episode directly to viewers who don't already follow the show. That's the algorithm actively deciding this topic and guest belonged in front of a wider audience, and it worked. Once people got past the first couple minutes, they stayed locked in. The stretch on dealing with haters and staying positive, and the section on favorite artists and having a supportive partner, both pulled viewers back in through the middle of the episode.

June also grew the channel's reach: views were up 5% to 2,263, and 774 people discovered the channel for the first time, nearly all of this month's audience was new. Your Shorts are doing real work pulling in that new reach, "Copy Paste Is Killing Tattooing" and "Never Say This Is Fine" were your two biggest pulls.

What Needs Work

We're going back through the show's catalog of older episodes to refresh packaging: new titles, descriptions, SEO, and thumbnails on episodes that already have strong content but haven't been discovered the way they deserve. Going forward, titles will be pulled directly from the story each episode actually tells, so the packaging always matches the conversation.

With that much new-viewer volume, the bigger opportunity is turning browsers into subscribers, net subscriber growth this month was only 12, down from May. The outros in your episodes right now aren't asking viewers to subscribe or follow, adding that one line will give all of this new traffic somewhere to go. Please record this as an outro (audio-only):

"Thanks for checking out our podcast. If you haven't done so already, hit that subscribe button to help support the channel and to keep us bringing great guests to you. I'm Austin Spencer... and I'll see you on the next one."

Buzzsprout now supports posting video versions of the podcast to Apple, the same way we're already doing on Spotify. 43% of your audio audience is on Spotify and 41% is on Apple, nearly as large an audience with no video option right now. We recommend upgrading the Buzzsprout plan to bring video to Apple while you're an early adopter of this rollout.
